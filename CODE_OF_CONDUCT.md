# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone.
Participants are expected to communicate professionally, assume good intent,
and focus criticism on ideas, code, documentation, and project outcomes.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and receive constructive feedback with technical specificity.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project.
- Credit people for their work and avoid misrepresenting authorship.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Trolling, insults, or sustained disruption of project work.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, restrict participation,
or ban contributors whose conduct is harmful to the project or community.
Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented when appropriate, and
focused on protecting contributors and users.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. Include relevant
links, screenshots, logs, dates, and a short description of what happened.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith and avoid unnecessary disclosure
of reporter identity or sensitive details.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project repositories, issue trackers, pull
requests, discussions, release channels, and any public or private space where
someone is representing the project.
