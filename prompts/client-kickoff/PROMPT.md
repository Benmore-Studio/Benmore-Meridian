Prepare a kickoff agenda and talking points for a new client engagement.

Client/Project: $1

Cover:
1. **Introductions** — Team, roles, communication preferences
2. **Project goals** — What success looks like, key metrics
3. **Scope** — Features in v1, what's deferred, decision criteria
4. **Timeline** — Milestones, sprint cadence, delivery dates
5. **Technical** — Stack decisions, hosting, integrations, constraints
6. **Process** — Standup schedule, PR review, demo cadence
7. **Risks** — Dependencies, unknowns, mitigation strategies
